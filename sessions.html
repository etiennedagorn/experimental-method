Course Content

Each session combines conceptual reasoning, empirical design, and a short base-R exercise. The topic numbers below follow the course programme. Use the combined teaching decks and the linked homework and R starters for each topic.

Which slides should I use? Sessions 2–3 have a continuation deck, building on the selection and matching background linked under Session 2. Sessions 5–6 share one teaching deck, and Sessions 7–8 share another. Revised topic-specific decks for Sessions 5–8 are available as reference material.
